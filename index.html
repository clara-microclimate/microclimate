使命 · Mission

Mission

我們設計生活中的空白，好讓 AI 以更友善多元包容的方式嵌入到我們的生活——透過部署 AI 事件在真實生活的場景，進行實驗、測繪人類對未來的想像。

Microclimate designs the negative space in everyday life — the deliberate room where AI can settle in, friendly, diverse, and inclusive. Through AI encounters staged in real-world scenarios, we map the human imagination of the future.

我們如何工作 · How We Work

How We Work

  • 01

    察

    Observe

    走進日常場景，辨認出可以留白的縫隙。

    We start inside everyday scenes, reading where a deliberate emptiness could open.

  • 02

    駐

    Stage

    讓 AI 以友善、多元、包容的姿態，暫居於這個空白之中。

    AI is invited to settle into that space — gently, diversely, without forcing itself.

  • 03

    繪

    Map

    記錄人們與 AI 相遇時，浮現出的未來想像。

    We map what surfaces when people and AI meet — the shape of imagined futures.
